# Logomarks

5,500+ free geometric logomarks at [logomarks.ai](https://logomarks.ai), free for any use, commercial included.

## Packages

The libraries behind the set, on npm:

- [`@logomarks/core`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@logomarks/core): geometry, analysis and similarity for marks
- [`@logomarks/editor`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@logomarks/editor): an in-browser vector editor for marks
- [`@logomarks/brand-kit`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@logomarks/brand-kit): a brand kit and social pack from a mark
- [`@logomarks/image-effects`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@logomarks/image-effects): image filters such as grading, grain, blur and halftone
- [`@logomarks/mockups`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@logomarks/mockups): product mockups from your artwork
- [`@logomarks/mockups-plates`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@logomarks/mockups-plates): photo scenes for the mockups
- [`@logomarks/shaders`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@logomarks/shaders): WebGL scenes such as marble, glass and chrome
